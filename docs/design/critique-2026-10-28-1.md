## Top fixes

**A Sibling card**
1. Desktop: widen the card to the 1100 px column and put the tools beside the cut (stage left, tools column right) so the desktop is designed, not a centred phone card.
2. Move Download PNG to the right end of the tool row, the same height as Keep/Erase, and put New photo next to the title instead of the status row.
3. QR/OCR links: give each a 44 px tall tap box (padding, `display:inline-flex;min-height:44px`).

**B Workbench**
1. Empty state: Download PNG outlined and disabled (no cyan fill) until a cut exists; one filled primary per view (R8).
2. Remove the rail "Choose photo" in the empty state, or make it the only one: keep the stage button and rename the rail slot "New photo" only after a cut (same action, one place at a time; nothing is lost).
3. Empty stage: group icon, text, button and hint into one 16 px-gap cluster at the centre instead of spreading them over 400 px; phone wordmark on one line (title 2 px smaller, New photo below it).

**C Compare**
1. Put New photo inside the toolbar strip, left of the status text, instead of alone under it.
2. QR/OCR links: 44 px tall tap boxes.
3. Empty desktop: drop zone to 320 px tall with its content in one centred cluster, so the empty view does not read as a 440 px void.
